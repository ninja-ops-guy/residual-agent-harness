# Contributing to RESIDUAL Open Core

Thank you for contributing.

## Open-core scope

Public contributions are accepted for paths listed in `docs/open-source/OPEN_SOURCE_MANIFEST.md`. Reserved commercial paths are not part of the public contribution surface unless a maintainer explicitly opens a task for them.

## Rights and provenance

By submitting a contribution for inclusion in the Apache-2.0 Open Core, you agree that the contribution is submitted under the Apache License 2.0 terms applicable to that work, including Section 5.

You must have the right to submit the contribution. Do not submit employer-confidential, customer-confidential, proprietary third-party, credential-bearing or unlawfully copied material.

## Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off

Commits intended for merge should carry a `Signed-off-by:` trailer using the contributor's real name and an email address they are authorized to use. The sign-off represents the Developer Certificate of Origin process described at https://developercertificate.org/.

Example:

`Signed-off-by: Jane Developer <jane@example.com>`

## Boundary changes

A pull request that adds/removes paths from the Open Source Manifest is a licensing-boundary change and requires explicit maintainer review. No automated agent may expand the open-source boundary on its own authority.

## AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted code is welcome, but the human submitter remains responsible for correctness, provenance, licensing and review. Material copied from another project must be identified and attributed with its applicable license.

## Research evidence

Research-result changes should preserve negative results, revision identifiers, experimental conditions and uncertainty. Do not rewrite failed experiments into successful narratives.
