# Authors and project credit

## Project creator and maintainer

**Mike Olivares** (`ninja-ops-guy`) is the creator, primary maintainer, and research lead of RESIDUAL.

The public `residual-agent-harness` repository was established on September 13, 2026. Git history, pull requests, frozen experiment artifacts, specifications, receipts, and release metadata provide the project’s primary public chronology.

## AI-assisted development

RESIDUAL is developed with extensive use of AI coding and research systems as tools. Those systems may generate proposals, code, reviews, test plans, analyses, or experimental material, but they do not replace the human maintainer’s responsibility for architecture, provenance, licensing, research claims, acceptance, or authorship decisions.

## External contributors

External human contributors should be credited for their actual contributions in Git history, release notes, papers, datasets, or other research artifacts as appropriate.

Contribution does not imply authorship of every RESIDUAL research result. Paper and artifact authorship should reflect substantive intellectual and research contribution.

## Citation

For software use, cite `CITATION.cff` at the exact version or commit used.

For research use, also cite the relevant experiment specification, frozen dataset/benchmark, evidence bundle, release, preprint, or DOI when one exists.
