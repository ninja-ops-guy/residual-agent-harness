# IP Indemnification (ENT8-R4)

Requirement: **ENT8-R4** — Residual Systems, Inc. MUST provide
indemnification for intellectual property infringement: if a third party
claims Residual infringes their patents, copyrights, or trade secrets, the
vendor MUST defend the customer and cover damages.

## Scope of Indemnification

Residual Systems, Inc. ("Vendor") will, at its own expense, defend the
customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Residual
platform — as delivered by Vendor and used in accordance with the
agreement and documentation — infringes that party's patents, copyrights,
or trade secrets, and will indemnify the customer against damages and
costs (including reasonable attorney fees) finally awarded or agreed in
settlement.

## Conditions

The obligation applies when the customer:

1. Notifies Vendor in writing within 30 days of the claim.
2. Grants Vendor sole control of the defense and settlement (the customer
   may participate with its own counsel at its own expense).
3. Provides reasonable cooperation.

## Remedies

If the platform is, or in Vendor's opinion is likely to become, subject to
an infringement claim, Vendor will, at its option:

1. Procure the right for the customer to continue using the platform; or
2. Modify or replace the affected component with a non-infringing
   equivalent of substantially similar functionality; or
3. If neither is commercially practicable, terminate the affected license
   and refund prepaid, unused fees.

## Exclusions (standard)

No obligation arises from: (a) customer modification of the platform;
(b) combination with third-party products not provided by Vendor, where
the claim arises from the combination; (c) use in violation of the
agreement; (d) customer-provided modules or content; (e) open-source core
used outside a commercial agreement (commercial indemnification attaches
to commercial licenses, ENT8-R7).

## Relationship to Other Protections

- Indemnification obligations are backed by Vendor's insurance program
  (ENT8-R5).
- Uncapped for IP indemnification unless the MSA states otherwise;
  liability-cap carve-out is standard.
