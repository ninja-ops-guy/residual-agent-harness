# Professional Services (ENT8-R3)

Requirement: **ENT8-R3** — Residual MUST offer professional services:
implementation assistance, custom module development, training delivery,
and integration development, available as fixed-scope engagements or
ongoing retainers.

## Service Catalog

### 1. Implementation Assistance
- Station deployment (single-node and HA/DR), IAM/SSO integration
  (SPEC-ENT-001), observability wiring.
- Architecture and security review board support: presenting the ENT7-R2
  and ENT7-R3 packages to customer boards.
- Pilot program operation per the ENT7-R1 framework, including rollback
  rehearsal and graduation evaluation.

### 2. Custom Module Development
- Customer-specific modules built to the contract-first conventions in
  `residual/`, delivered with tests, manifests, and signatures.
- Handoff includes developer training (ENT7-R5 developer track) so the
  customer can maintain the module.

### 3. Training Delivery
- All four role curricula (ENT7-R5): operators, administrators,
  developers, auditors — delivered on-site or remote.
- Every delivery includes the sandbox environment required for hands-on
  exercises (ENT7-R6); we do not offer lecture-only training.

### 4. Integration Development
- Connectors to CRM/ERP/ticketing/data platforms via the integration
  layer, with observation receipts for every external call (ENT6-R7).

## Engagement Models

| Model | Terms |
|---|---|
| **Fixed-scope engagement** | SOW with defined deliverables, milestones, and acceptance criteria; fixed price; change orders via CAB |
| **Ongoing retainer** | Monthly bucket of engineer-days (10/20/40); priority scheduling; rollover up to 20% for 90 days |

## Rates (list)

- Senior engineer: $2,200/day · Staff engineer: $2,800/day
- Training delivery: $9,500 per cohort-day (max 12 attendees), sandbox
  included.
- Educational/non-profit discounts apply per the licensing policy
  (ENT8-R7).

## Acceptance

Deliverables are accepted against objective criteria — typically passing
module validation gates and green test suites — receipted where produced
by Residual itself.
