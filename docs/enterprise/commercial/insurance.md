# Insurance Program (ENT8-R5)

Requirement: **ENT8-R5** — Residual MUST carry cyber liability insurance
(minimum $5M), errors and omissions insurance (minimum $2M), and general
liability insurance (minimum $1M); a certificate of insurance MUST be
available on request.

All carriers hold A.M. Best rating A- or better. Bracketed fields are
completed on the certificate of insurance (COI) issued to each requester.

## Coverage Notes

- **Cyber liability ($5M)**: covers data breaches, incident response
  costs, notification, regulatory fines where insurable, and network
  security liability — relevant to the DPA breach-notification
  obligations (ENT8-R8).
- **Errors & omissions ($2M)**: covers failure of the platform or
  professional services (ENT8-R3) to perform as contracted, and supports
  the IP indemnification obligation (ENT8-R4) where insurable.
- **General liability ($1M/occurrence, $2M aggregate)**: bodily injury and
  property damage, including on-site services under Enterprise support
  (ENT8-R2).

## Certificate of Insurance (COI) Process

1. Request via the account manager or legal@ contact.
2. COI issued within 5 business days, naming the customer as certificate
   holder; additional-insured status available for GL where required.
3. 30-day notice of cancellation or material reduction is provided to
   certificate holders.
4. Renewal evidence is refreshed annually and before each contract
   renewal; lapse of any required coverage is a contractual breach.

## Customer Verification

Customers may verify coverage during procurement due diligence alongside
financial stability evidence (ENT8-R6). Current certificates are attached
to the vendor due-diligence bundle.
