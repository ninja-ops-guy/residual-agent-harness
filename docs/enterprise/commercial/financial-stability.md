# Financial Stability Evidence (ENT8-R6)

Requirement: **ENT8-R6** — Residual MUST provide financial stability
evidence: funding history, revenue or burn rate, runway projection, and
customer count, sufficient to support a reasonable belief that the vendor
will exist in 3 years.

This document defines the evidence structure; current figures are provided
under NDA in the due-diligence bundle and refreshed quarterly.

Total capital raised: $[X]M. Cap table available under NDA; no debt
obligations senior to customer commitments.

## 2. Revenue / Burn Rate

- **ARR**: $[X]M, growing [X]% YoY, from commercial licenses (ENT8-R7),
  support tiers (ENT8-R2), and professional services (ENT8-R3).
- **Gross margin**: [X]% (software-weighted mix).
- **Net burn**: $[X]K/month average over trailing 6 months (or:
  cash-flow positive since [date]).
- **Concentration**: top customer < 15% of ARR; top 5 < 40%.

## 3. Runway Projection

| Scenario | Assumption | Runway |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Current burn, current ARR growth | [X] months |
| Conservative | Zero new sales, current burn | [X] months |
| Stress | 30% churn, 20% cost increase | [X] months |

Runway in all scenarios MUST exceed 36 months at signing of any
multi-year agreement; if it falls below, escrow provisions (below)
activate.

## 4. Customer Count

- Paying commercial customers: [N], across [industries].
- Logos and reference calls available under NDA.

## 5. Continuity Protections

- **Source-code escrow**: quarterly deposits with [escrow agent];
  release on insolvency, breach of support obligations, or discontinuation
  of the product. Open-core licensing already limits lock-in (ENT8-R7).
- **Key-person and D&O insurance** maintained as part of the insurance
  program (ENT8-R5).
- **3-year commitment statement**: signed by the CFO with each
  enterprise MSA, backed by this evidence pack.

## 6. Update Cadence and Audit

Evidence refreshed quarterly; annual reviewed financials (audit when ARR
exceeds $10M). Material adverse changes disclosed to enterprise customers
within 30 days.
