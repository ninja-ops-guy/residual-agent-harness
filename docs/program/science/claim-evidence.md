No new empirical result is asserted by this preparation. A future claim ledger
must bind exact protocol, workload, run bundle, analysis implementation, model,
source commit/tree and externally retained digest. A filename by itself is not
evidence that an artifact exists or that a property passed.

The earlier Verified Cyber Planning/CIC/LDD work is architectural lineage. Cite
its own archived artifacts and provenance if discussed; it does not supply new
Residual empirical observations. Distinguish literature, prior result, mechanism,
hypothesis and new result in manuscript edits. Every new-result sentence needs a
claim ledger row and an externally reproducible numerical provenance chain.

Adverse evidence belongs in the same ledger: false acceptances, verifier misses,
failed containment, slower/costlier swarms, capacity ordering reversals, unresolved
comparisons and aborted runs must remain visible. Figure scaffolding has no
evidentiary weight and cannot be cited as a measured result.
