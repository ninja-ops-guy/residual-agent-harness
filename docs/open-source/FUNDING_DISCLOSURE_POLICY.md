# Funding disclosure policy

RESIDUAL may seek research and open-source support without disclosing unnecessary proprietary product information.

## Safe to disclose in open-source funding applications

- public repository and open-core scope
- published architecture already present in the repository
- public research questions and evaluation methodology
- public benchmarks, verifiers and reproducibility tooling
- use of model/API credits for open-source experiments, maintenance and evaluation
- aggregate development activity that can be verified

## Do not disclose solely for a funding application

- unreleased proprietary implementation details
- non-public enterprise roadmap
- confidential commercial pricing/negotiations
- private customer data or prospect information
- employer-confidential material
- unpublished secrets, credentials or security-sensitive deployment details
- patent-sensitive inventions before an IP strategy review

## Funding allocation

Unless an agreement expressly says otherwise, credits or grants represented as support for RESIDUAL Open Core should be consumed by open-source maintenance, evaluation, reproducibility, model comparisons, benchmark execution and openly documented research.
