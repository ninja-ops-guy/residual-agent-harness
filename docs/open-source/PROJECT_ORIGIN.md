## Purpose of this record

This document preserves a public, version-controlled record of RESIDUAL’s project identity and research provenance. It is intended to make chronology, authorship, and artifact lineage easier to verify independently.

It does not claim ownership over broad ideas that are not protectable, nor does it imply that independently developed similar systems derive from RESIDUAL.

## Project thesis

RESIDUAL investigates whether reliable system behavior can be improved by constraining, observing, verifying, and deterministically integrating nondeterministic AI-generated work rather than relying solely on the reliability of any individual model.

The project develops engineering and research infrastructure around explicit worker contracts, evidence, verification, receipts, bounded authority, failure handling, evaluation, and multi-agent coordination.

## Priority evidence

Priority and chronology should be established from immutable or independently verifiable artifacts where possible, including:

- Git commits and signed/versioned releases;
- pull requests and issue history;
- frozen experiment specifications and preregistrations;
- content-addressed evidence bundles and digests;
- benchmark/data manifests;
- retained negative results and failed trials;
- public preprints, papers, archived releases, and DOI-backed artifacts when available.

No single prose document is authoritative for priority when stronger revision-bound evidence exists.

## Research claims

Research claims should identify:

2. the experiment/protocol revision;
3. the data or benchmark revision;
4. the model/provider configuration when relevant;
5. the verification criteria;
6. the retained evidence bundle or digest;
7. limitations, failures, and operator interventions.
