# IP and provenance record

This file establishes a provenance discipline for RESIDUAL. It is an engineering record, not a substitute for legal advice.

## Maintainer origin

The project is currently maintained by Mike Olivares. AI coding/research systems are used as development tools under maintainer direction and review.

## Required provenance for accepted contributions

A contribution must:

1. be authored or submitted by someone with the right to contribute it;
2. not contain confidential employer, customer, client or third-party material;
3. identify copied/adapted third-party code and its license;
4. preserve required copyright/license notices;
5. use the repository contribution sign-off process;
6. not move code across the open/commercial boundary without an explicit boundary review.

## Employment/IP separation

RESIDUAL development must remain separated from employer-owned code, credentials, non-public datasets, internal documentation, proprietary workflows and other confidential materials.

Before material commercialization, the maintainer should obtain qualified counsel review of applicable employment/invention-assignment obligations and the final licensing structure.

## AI-assisted development

AI assistance does not waive provenance requirements. The maintainer remains responsible for reviewing generated contributions, avoiding unauthorized copying, and retaining evidence of source and review when a contribution is material.

## Current limitation

This record is not an independent historical IP audit. It establishes the policy going forward and flags legal review as a pre-commercialization gate.
