RESIDUAL / CONNECT A PROVIDER

This optional panel connects Puter outside the Linux guest. Loading its software contacts an external service with your IP and browser information and may enable provider-managed cookies or storage. Signing in and sending a model request are separate actions.

Authorized model calls transmit your prompt, selected source snapshots, and related request context to Puter and the selected model service. The bridge is designed not to pass passwords or authentication tokens into the guest. Provider credits or charges may apply, even if a call times out. Do not send secrets, sensitive information, or confidential employer or customer material.
