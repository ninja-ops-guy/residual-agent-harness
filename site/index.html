RELIABILITY CONTROL PLANE / UNTRUSTED COMPUTATION

Models provide intelligence. They do not get authority.

RESIDUAL is not another agent orchestration framework. It is an evidence-driven control plane for deciding whether the output of unreliable computation deserves to become accepted state.

THE CORE BOUNDARY: workers may plan, code, reason, explore, and repair. They cannot certify their own success. Contracts constrain execution. Evidence records facts. Verifiers decide acceptance. A deterministic integrator controls state.

Hypothesis: reliable AI systems may not require perfectly reliable AI components. Reliability can emerge from constraining, observing, verifying, residualizing, and deterministically integrating unreliable computation.
WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

The durable unit of progress is verified work—not an agent run.

Most agent systems optimize how work is delegated and executed. RESIDUAL focuses on the harder boundary after execution: what evidence is sufficient to accept a result, what remains unresolved, and what may safely enter system state.

Conventional agent loop

Model executes → claims completion
Failure → retry or rerun substantial work
Logs/context describe what happened
Agent/workflow may drive integration
Reliability strongly follows worker reliability

RESIDUAL

Worker proposes → external verifier decides
Failure → repair unresolved residual only
Evidence establishes observed facts
Deterministic policy controls integration
Reliability is a property of the control plane
Example: 20 obligations → 19 independently verified → 1 fails. RESIDUAL preserves the 19 accepted obligations and creates residual R₁={failed obligation}. It does not make already-correct work probabilistic again by blindly recomputing it.
CONTROL PLANE

A worker saying “PASS” proves nothing.

Observed tool results, writes, hashes, budgets and test outcomes remain separate from model assertions.

VERIFIERS ARE ALSO FALLIBLE

Verification quality is measurable.

Verifier revision, precision, recall, coverage and calibration can themselves become evidence and policy inputs.

AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

The LLM cannot merge itself.

Only artifacts satisfying verifier and policy requirements become eligible for deterministic integration.
