EVIDENCE-FIRST AI SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

Build reliable systems from unreliable computation.

RESIDUAL is an orchestration and verification harness for AI-assisted engineering. Workers propose. Verifiers decide. Evidence survives every handoff. Deterministic integration controls what becomes accepted state.

Start here ↗Read the working paper
Research hypothesis: AI reliability does not necessarily require making each individual model reliable. Reliability can emerge from constraining, observing, verifying, and deterministically integrating unreliable computation.
SYSTEM MODEL

The model is replaceable compute. The harness owns authority.

RESIDUAL moves correctness authority out of the worker and into explicit contracts, independent verification, evidence, policy, and deterministic acceptance boundaries.

RESEARCH PROGRAM

A harness designed to make the reliability claim falsifiable.

The project separates implemented mechanisms, development evidence, hypotheses, and conclusions that still require controlled study. Evaluation is built around paired trials, ablations, fault injection, model degradation, hidden grading, and matched baselines.

CLAIM DISCIPLINE

Unknown is not pass.

FAIL, UNKNOWN, malformed output, verifier exceptions, provider errors, and abstention remain visible. The system is designed to reject unsupported success rather than smooth it over.
