Information and purposes

Website delivery. Your browser sends network and device information when retrieving pages and assets. GitHub states that GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. GitHub's handling is described in its privacy statement. This project website does not configure advertising pixels, cross-site marketing profiles, or a first-party analytics collection endpoint.

Walkthrough. Tab selection and walkthrough progress stay in the current page by default. Choosing “Remember walkthrough progress” saves that state in this browser for up to 180 days from your choice. Unchecking it removes that saved preference and progress. These values are not sent to a RESIDUAL analytics service. See storage and choices for the keys and controls.

Browser VM. Requested VM operation uses browser storage and runtime resources for the guest disk, local artifacts, and restart state. Mission prompts, selected source snapshots, generated files, model responses, and execution evidence may be present in the guest, browser-local mission or conversation history, and downloaded evidence. Conversation continuity can use browser-local storage across reloads; do not assume closing a tab erases a conversation. A fresh guest is not proof of erasing older disk data. {{runtime_disclosure}}

Correspondence. When you contact the operator, the operator receives your contact details and the information you choose to send, and uses them to respond, investigate security or privacy issues, and keep necessary records. The published contact address uses Google Gmail for email delivery and storage; see Google's privacy policy. Your own email provider may also handle the message. Public GitHub issues and contributions are public: do not use them for personal-data requests or sensitive reports.

Optional AI provider: Puter

Provider setup first asks you to choose whether to load Puter's external software. Loading it contacts Puter and may expose IP address, browser information, and provider-managed storage or session information. Signing in is a separate action. The provider bridge is designed not to pass passwords or authentication tokens into the Linux guest; the provider's software still runs in the browser panel.

Before a model run, Mission Control separately asks you to authorize the prompt and selected source snapshots. Authorized calls send model-request context to Puter and the selected upstream model service, and return responses and usage information. Provider allowances or charges can apply, including when a request times out. Read Puter's privacy policy, Puter's terms, and the terms applicable to your chosen model. We do not promise provider-controlled retention, deletion, or training restrictions.

Keep the provider disconnected to avoid this optional processing. In the open setup panel, use “Disconnect provider” to unload that panel's provider software. Close or reload a connected demo to end that page's connection. These actions do not undo submitted requests, charges, or provider-held records; they are not account deletion or guaranteed sign-out everywhere.

Retention and deletion

Unremembered walkthrough state lasts until the page is closed or reloaded. Remembered walkthrough state expires after 180 days and is removed on the next visit, or sooner when you withdraw the choice. VM storage and browser caches can remain until you delete them through your browser; downloaded files remain under your control. Provider and host records follow those services' applicable retention rules.

The operator keeps correspondence only as long as needed to address the request, maintain necessary security or dispute records, and meet applicable legal requirements. Relevant factors include whether the matter is resolved, the sensitivity of the information, and any required retention period. A request for deletion will be assessed against these purposes and legal requirements; it is not a promise to erase records controlled by another service.
