Storage and privacy choices

Effective date: {{effective_date}}

The public site's own advertising and marketing analytics are not enabled. Optional functionality is controlled where you use it, rather than by an “accept everything” banner.

Walkthrough storage: optional

The walkthrough runs in memory by default. On the walkthrough page, choose “Remember walkthrough progress” to save the selected tab, steps, and run state in localStorage under residual.walkthrough.v1. The choice and expiry are stored under residual.walkthrough.consent.v1. Both expire 180 days after you choose to remember, and are removed on the next visit after expiry. There is no automatic renewal. You can still use the walkthrough when storage is unavailable or remembering is off.

Uncheck the choice on the walkthrough page, or use this control to forget the saved choice and progress on this browser. This only removes those two RESIDUAL walkthrough keys.

Browser VM: requested local operation

Opening the browser demo loads the resources necessary to operate its Linux guest. Browser caches, IndexedDB-backed disk storage, and the runtime's own storage may persist after you leave. Browser-local conversation continuity may also retain mission history and a current conversation identifier across reloads. The session key residual.guest.generation.v1 identifies a restarted guest overlay. Restarting the guest selects a fresh overlay; it does not prove that old overlays or caches were erased. {{runtime_disclosure}}

To remove VM data, close other RESIDUAL tabs and use your browser's site-data settings for ninja-ops-guy.github.io. That origin is shared with other projects on the same GitHub Pages account: clearing all its data can affect those projects too. Browser settings may clear cookies, local storage, IndexedDB, caches, and service workers together. Delete downloaded evidence separately through your device's file manager.

Provider software: a separate choice

Puter software is not loaded by the provider panel until you explicitly click “Load Puter” after its point-of-collection notice. The choice is not remembered by RESIDUAL across panel reloads. The provider may use its own cookies or storage after loading; review Puter's privacy policy. Signing in and authorizing a model run are separate actions. A saved provider session does not skip this page's load choice.

Use “Disconnect provider” in the open setup panel to reload that panel without loading the SDK. Close or reload the demo to disconnect a hidden panel. This stops new calls through that page but cannot recall requests already sent, erase provider records, revoke all provider account sessions, or reverse charges. Use the provider's account controls for those requests.

Hosting, signals, and changes

GitHub and runtime asset services necessarily receive network requests to serve requested resources. Storage does not become advertising-free merely because it is called a cache; the operator reviews each deployment's actual services and purposes. See browser signals and third-party practices. No first-party sale or targeted-advertising function is enabled. New trackers, embedded media, analytics, or providers require a new inventory and policy review before activation.
